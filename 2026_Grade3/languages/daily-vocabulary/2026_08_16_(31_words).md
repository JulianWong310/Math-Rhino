**curb**（路缘、约束、控制）：The edge of a sidewalk, or to control.

**governor**（州长、总督、主管）：The leader of a state or region.

**dainty**（精致的、娇小的、秀丽的）：Small, pretty, and delicate.

**excuse**（借口、原谅、宽恕）：A reason given to explain a mistake, or to forgive.

**irritable**（易怒的、急躁的）：Getting annoyed or angry easily.

**overlooked**（忽视、俯瞰、忽略）：Failed to notice, or had a view from above.

**balcony**（阳台、楼座）：A platform sticking out from a building upstairs.

**boar**（野猪）：A wild male pig.

**winced**（畏缩、皱眉、退缩）：Moved back suddenly in pain or fear.

**fringe**（刘海、边缘、流苏）：Hair hanging over your forehead, or an edge of something.

**margins**（边缘、页边距、利润）：Empty space at the edge of a page.

**attempting**（尝试、试图）：Trying to do something.

**examining**（检查、审查）：Looking at something carefully.

**leisurely**（悠闲的、从容的、慢悠悠的）：Slow and relaxed.

**stroll**（散步、闲逛）：To take a slow, relaxed walk.

**idle**（闲散的、闲置的、懒散的）：Not working or active.

**expression**（表达、表情、措辞）：Showing a feeling through words or face.

**sincere**（真诚的、诚挚的、真实的）：Honest and genuine.

**praised**（赞扬、称赞、赞美）：Said good things about someone.

**perky**（活泼的、精神饱满的）：Cheerful and full of energy.

**vetoed**（否决、禁止）：Rejected a decision or law, stopping it from happening.

**stupefaction**（麻木、惊愕、神志不清）：The state of being very confused or shocked.
