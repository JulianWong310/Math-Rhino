**gauze**（纱布、薄纱）：A thin, see-through fabric used for bandages or curtains.

**decanter**（醒酒器、玻璃水瓶）：A decorative bottle used to hold wine or other drinks.

**trek**（艰苦跋涉、长途徒步）：A long, difficult walk.

**expedition**（远征、探险）：A journey with a specific purpose.

**drapery**（布料、帷幔、布艺品）：Cloth hung in folds, often used as curtains.

**shutter**（百叶窗、快门）：A window cover that opens and closes, or the part of a camera that opens to let in light.

**gala**（庆典、盛会）：A big, fancy event or party.

**fiesta**（节日、狂欢节）：A big celebration or party.

**shortening**（起酥油、缩短）：A fat used in baking to make dough crumbly.

**margarine**（人造黄油）：A butter-like spread made from vegetable oils.

**masseuse**（女按摩师）：A woman who gives massages.

**ringmaster**（马戏团主持人）：The person who introduces acts in a circus.

**lion tamer**（驯狮师）：A person who trains and controls lions.

**disclose**（揭露、透露）：To make secret information known.

**literature**（文学、文学作品）：Written works, especially those considered art.

**smallpox**（天花）：A serious disease that caused spots and scars.

**measles**（麻疹）：A contagious disease with red spots on the skin.

**influenza**（流行性感冒、流感）：A common, contagious illness with fever and cough.

**pew**（教堂长椅）：A long bench with a back, used in a church.

**knack**（窍门、技巧、习惯）：A special talent or clever way of doing something.

**dormitory**（宿舍）：A large building where many students sleep.

**domicile**（住所、住宅）：A place where you live.

**ramekin**（小烤碗、小盅）：A small baking dish for individual portions.

**gargle**（漱口、含漱）：To wash your throat with liquid by breathing through it.

**gallbladder**（胆囊）：An organ that stores liquid waste from the liver.

**femur**（股骨）：The bone in your upper leg.

**pancreas**（胰腺）：An organ that helps digest food.

**mauled**（抓伤、撕裂、袭击）：Hurt badly by an animal.

**scowled**（皱眉、怒视）：Made an angry face.

**chairman**（主席、董事长）：The leader of a committee or meeting.

**aviator**（飞行员）：A person who flies an aircraft.

**Kwanzaa**（宽扎节）：An African-American holiday celebrated from December 26 to January 1.

**cardigan**（开襟羊毛衫）：A knitted sweater that opens at the front.

**hunch**（预感、驼背）：A feeling that something might happen, or a curved back.

**suspicion**（怀疑、猜疑）：A feeling that someone might be guilty.

**assurance**（保证、确信）：A promise that something will happen.

**intuition**（直觉）：The ability to know something without thinking.

**tempo**（速度、节奏）：The speed at which music is played.

**foyer**（门厅、大堂）：An entrance hall of a building.

**lobby**（大厅、游说）：An entrance area, or to persuade lawmakers.

**cinder**（煤渣、灰烬）：A small piece of burnt wood or coal.
